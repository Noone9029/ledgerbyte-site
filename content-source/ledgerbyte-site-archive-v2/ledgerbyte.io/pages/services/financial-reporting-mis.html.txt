What is the difference between bookkeeping and management reporting? Bookkeeping records what happened. Management reporting translates that financial data into a recurring pack that shows what changed, why it matters, and where leadership should focus next. How often should management reporting be prepared? For most growing businesses, monthly reporting is the right cadence. It is frequent enough to support decisions without creating reporting noise every week. Can you help if our reporting exists but is not useful? Yes. Many businesses already produce reports, but the issue is structure, timing, commentary quality, or a mismatch between the report and the decisions leadership actually needs to make.
