# LedgerByte Accounting Search Strategy

## Objective

LedgerByte cannot credibly rank for every search containing “accounting” with
one page. The site should own a connected set of UAE commercial intents, with
one canonical page per intent and expert-reviewed supporting content. Keywords
below are page topics and measurement clusters, not phrases to repeat
mechanically.

## Current Search Baseline

Search Console data for 2026-04-29 through 2026-07-28 shows that the new
`/finance/*` routes have not yet accumulated query data. The clearest non-brand
signal is the existing monthly-reporting article:

- `bookkeeping monthly reporting`: 60 impressions, average position 23.5
- `monthly financial reporting bookkeeping`: 58 impressions, position 17.4
- `working capital forecasting software`: 49 impressions across the retired
  cash-flow URL variants, position about 42.6

This means the first opportunity is to strengthen proven reporting demand while
Google discovers the new service URLs.

## Content Queue

Each finance article requires a named author, a qualified finance reviewer,
official sources, an accurate update date, and contextual links to the relevant
service pages.

1. Refresh the existing monthly financial reporting article around the two
   Search Console queries above. Add a practical monthly close and reporting
   cadence, then link naturally to bookkeeping and MIS services.
2. Publish a UAE bookkeeping scope guide covering monthly deliverables,
   reconciliation, catch-up work, software handover, timing, and pricing
   factors.
3. Publish a VAT and corporate-tax readiness guide citing current official FTA
   material. Do not publish unreviewed regulatory advice.
4. Publish a WPS responsibilities guide that clearly distinguishes payroll
   calculation, SIF preparation, WPS coordination, and regulated salary
   disbursement.
5. Publish a fractional CFO versus bookkeeper versus finance manager guide.
6. Publish a cash-flow forecast template and a guide distinguishing advisory
   work from forecasting software.
7. Publish an impartial UAE accounting-automation comparison based on genuine
   implementation experience.

## Authority and Measurement

- Seek verified links from software partners, professional associations,
  chambers, founder communities, reputable UAE directories, and real clients.
- Publish one verified case study and one practical finance asset per quarter.
- Do not buy links, create thin Dubai/Sharjah city clones, or add a meta
  keywords tag.
- Review indexing weekly until all priority finance pages are indexed.
- Track non-brand impressions, top-20 queries, qualified organic enquiries,
  WhatsApp clicks, calls, and forms. Set growth targets after the first complete
  28-day post-launch baseline.
